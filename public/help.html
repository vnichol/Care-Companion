How to use Care Companion

Care keeps track of medicines, appointments and how the patient is feeling, and sends reminders to your phones. Everyone who opens it with the family code sees the same information.

Care is a helper, not a doctor. Always follow the care team's instructions. For a fever, a warning sign, or anything that worries you, call the team. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in North America).

Getting started

  1. Open the Care web address in Safari on the iPhone. (The very first time, Care asks who it’s for and makes the family code. See the setup guide.)
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Care from the new Home Screen icon. Enter the family code and your first name, then tap Open.

You only enter the code once on each phone. Your name is shown next to the things you log, so you can tell who checked off a dose.

The patient's phone and a caregiver's phone can both use Care at the same time. Changes on one phone show up on the other within about a minute, or right away when you reopen the app.

Keep the family code private. Anyone with it can see and change the information in Care.

Reminders

On iPhone, reminders only work when Care is opened from the Home Screen icon.

  1. Go to Settings and tap Turn on reminders, then allow notifications.
  2. Tap Send a test to check that a notification arrives.
  3. Choose what this phone gets:
    • Medication times and refills: a notification when a dose is due, and when supply is running low.
    • Appointments: a reminder before each visit.
    • Caregiver alerts: when a dose isn't checked off within 30 minutes, and right away when a fever, warning sign, or blood in the stool is logged. Turn this on for caregivers' phones.
    • Pain check-ins: a reminder to rate pain at the times set in Settings.

Doses in the same blister pack slot (for example Breakfast) come as one notification.

The Today screen

Today shows large buttons (tiles) for the things you log most, followed by today's doses and upcoming appointments.

  • Log today's symptoms (at the very top, in colour until it's done): the daily check-in.
  • Symptom (wide): start a timer for a symptom.
  • Pain, Medicine, Temperature, Bowels, Water, Exercise.

Use the ‹ and › arrows at the top to look at other days. On a past day, use + Log a dose on this day to fill in something you forgot.

A tile turns red when something needs attention, such as a recent fever or three days without a bowel movement.

Checking off doses

Scheduled doses are listed in time order. Tap Taken when a dose is done, or Skip if it was skipped on purpose. The bar at the top shows how many of today's doses are done.

Blister packs show as one row per slot (Breakfast, Lunch, Supper, Bedtime), so one tap on Taken checks off the whole pocket. Tap the row to see the medicines in it. Undo reverses a check-off.

Got the time wrong? Tap Change under a taken dose to fix the time it was taken, or the number of pills. On a blister pack, Change moves the whole pack to the new time. Use the ‹ arrow at the top of Today to fix a dose from an earlier day.

Tap the ⓘ next to a medicine's name for a plain-language explanation of what it's for and what to watch for.

Logging a medicine

Use the Medicine tile for as-needed doses (such as breakthrough hydromorphone) or an extra dose.

  1. Tap Medicine and pick the medicine from the list. It's in coloured groups (as needed, on a schedule, given at the clinic), A to Z within each.
  2. Set how many pills or how much was taken.
  3. If it was taken earlier, for example during the night, change the time.
  4. Tap Save.

Care shows when the last dose was taken, and warns you if it's too soon or over the daily limit set for that medicine. These doses appear under Other doses on Today, where Change fixes the date, time or amount.

Blister-pack medicines are folded away at the bottom of the list, because checking off the pack on Today already counts them. Only log one here for an extra pill outside the pack.

Pain

Tap Pain and choose a number from 0 (no pain) to 10 (the worst pain). You can add a note, such as where it hurts or what helped, and change the time if it was earlier. Pain can be rated any time, including when waking up at night.

If pain check-in reminders are on, a notification asks for a rating at the set times. The reminder is skipped if pain was rated in the last hour.

Symptoms (with a timer)

Use this for things like a back spasm, nausea, dizziness or a headache.

  1. Tap the Symptom tile, pick what it is (or type something else), and choose Minor or Major.
  2. Optional: where it is, a note, and when it started.
  3. Choose how to save it:
    • Start timer while it's happening. The tile shows a running clock; tap Stop when it ends.
    • Tap a length (1 min, 5 min, …) if it's already over.
    • Just log it (no time) if you don't know how long it lasted.

Temperature and fever

Tap Temperature and enter the reading in °C. If it's at or above the fever level (38.0 °C unless you changed it in Settings), Care shows a red Call the care team now screen with call buttons and alerts caregivers' phones.

A fever during myeloma treatment can be serious. Call the number the team gave you right away; don't wait to see if it goes down.

Bowels

Tap Bowels to record a bowel movement. Optionally pick what it was like on the 7-type scale (1 hard lumps, 4 smooth, 7 watery), the amount, and whether there was straining or pain. Save alone is enough.

  • The tile shows today's count. After 3 days with none it turns red. Pain medicines often cause constipation, so tell the team.
  • Ticking Blood seen or Black or tarry opens the call screen and alerts caregivers.

Water and exercise

Water: tap a container (glass, Yeti…) to add a full one, or type an amount in mL for part of one (for example half a Yeti). Set up containers and a daily goal in Settings. The bar on the tile shows progress toward the goal.

Exercise: add minutes of walking or other activity. Short walks count. The daily goal is set in Settings.

Daily check-in

Once a day, tap Log today's symptoms at the top of Today and answer a few quick questions: tiredness, numbness or tingling, nausea, shortness of breath, bowels, sleep, appetite, mood, and where it hurts. Symptoms go from None to Very bad; sleep, appetite and mood go from Very good to Very poor. It takes under a minute, and doctors find the pattern over time useful.

At the bottom are warning signs to watch for. Ticking any of them shows the call screen and alerts caregivers. You can tap the tile again to update today's answers.

Meds tab

The Meds tab lists every medicine, grouped as blister pack, other scheduled, as needed, given at the clinic, and on hold, A to Z within each group.

  • Tap Add a medication or tap a medicine to change it.
  • Fill in the name, dose and unit (mg, mL, puffs…), and the times. For a blister pack, choose the slot for each time.
  • Choose the days: every day, certain weekdays, or every N days (for example lenalidomide every 2 days) from a start date. Add an end date if the course stops.
  • As needed medicines have no reminders; you can set the minimum hours between doses and a daily maximum.
  • Enter the number of pills on hand to get a refill warning when supply is low.
  • Status: Active, Confirm (check with the pharmacist), or On hold (no reminders).

Importing a CSV

If the pharmacy or clinic gives you a medication list as a spreadsheet, save it as a CSV file and use Meds → Import from a CSV file.

  1. Choose the file. Care shows a preview: which medicines are new, which update an existing one, and any problems.
  2. Check the list, then tap Import. Nothing changes until you do.

Medicines are matched by name, so importing a newer list updates what's there instead of adding duplicates. Blister slot codes BK, LU, SP and BD (or HS) mean Breakfast, Lunch, Supper and Bedtime.

Visits and calendar

The Visits tab has two parts, switched at the top: Appointments and Circle of care.

  • Add an appointment with the date, time, place, who it's with, and notes (questions to ask, what to bring).
  • The month calendar marks days with appointments. Tap a date to see that day's appointments below it; use the arrows to change months.
  • Below the calendar are All upcoming and Recent appointments. Tap one to change or delete it.
  • The next few appointments also appear on Today under Coming up.

In your phone's Calendar: go to Settings → Subscribe in Calendar. Care appointments then show in the iPhone Calendar app and update on their own.

Circle of care

Visits → Circle of care holds everyone on the team: hematologist, oncology nurse, nurse navigator, pharmacist, family doctor, home care, and others.

  • Tap + Add and enter the name, role, clinic, phone, after-hours number, email and notes.
  • Each person has one-tap Call, After hours and Email buttons.
  • Tick Call for fevers & warning signs for the clinic or on-call line. Their numbers appear on the red call screen.
Add the cancer centre's daytime and after-hours numbers first, and mark them for fevers and warning signs.

Notes

Pick how the patient is feeling today and write anything worth remembering: side effects, questions for the doctor, what helped. Notes are kept by day and are included in the report.

Report for the doctor

The Report tab has two views:

  • Charts & log: pick a day, week or month, and all medicines or one. Shows medicine use (one bar per medicine), pain, water, exercise, temperature and symptoms as charts, then a day-by-day list of everything logged.
  • Visit summary: a one-page summary since the last appointment, with warning signs at the top, doses missed, as-needed use, pain, fevers, symptoms, bowels, and a Questions to ask list you can add to before the visit. Choose the period at the top.

Tap Share to send it as text, or Print or PDF to print it or save a PDF to bring along.

Settings

  • Your name on this device.
  • Screen: Day (light), Night (dark), or Auto to follow the phone. Each phone chooses its own.
  • Reminders on this device and the list of phones getting reminders.
  • Appointments in your Calendar.
  • Care team: link to the Circle of care, the fever level, and °C or °F.
  • Water containers and goal, Exercise goal, Pain check-in times.
  • Time zone: reminder times follow this. If you travel, Care offers to switch.
  • Sign out on this device. This doesn't delete anything.

When to call the care team

Settings → When to call the care team lists the signs to call about. In short, call right away for:

  • A temperature at or above the fever level, or shaking chills.
  • New back pain with leg weakness or numbness.
  • A swollen or painful leg, chest pain, or sudden shortness of breath.
  • Unusual bleeding or bruising, or blood or black stool.
  • Confusion or extreme drowsiness.

Use the list the team gave you if it's different.

Questions

Do I need the internet?

Care opens without a connection and shows the last information it had, but logging and reminders need the internet.

I'm not getting reminders.

Make sure Care was opened from the Home Screen icon, reminders are on in Settings, and notifications for Care are allowed in the iPhone's Settings → Notifications. Use Send a test. If it still doesn't work, tap Turn off, then turn reminders on again.

I made a mistake.

Most entries have a Remove or ✕ button. Remove it and log it again.

Something looks out of date.

Close Care completely and reopen it. It checks for new information and app updates each time it opens.

Is the information private?

It's stored in a private database that only opens with the family code. The calendar link shows appointments only and can't change anything.
